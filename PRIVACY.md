# Privacy policy

Mark Codex is a Windows and Mac desktop app maintained by Mark Gally Reboton.

The app uses:

- your microphone
- the speech or text you enter
- files in a folder you choose
- your sign-in to Codex, OpenAI, or Claude, when you connect one of those services

Speech, text, and selected files are sent only to the service you sign in to so the assistant can reply. The Windows setup can also download the Codex runtime to your PC. Mark Codex does not sell this information.

Conversation data stays on your computer except for what you send to the service you choose. That service handles the information under its own privacy policy.

You can stop the microphone, skip opening a folder, and sign out of the connected service at any time.
